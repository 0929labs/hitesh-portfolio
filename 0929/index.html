What is this
09 = birthday  ·  29 = the day this existed

0929 Labs is the other half. The engineering portfolio is at hiteshraj.in — structured, mechanical, serious. This is everything else.

Websites built properly for people who want something different. Photography of cars, events, cities, and landscapes — some of which got noticed by the right people. Vibe coding that turns ideas into real things without waiting months to learn how. All of it connected by the same stubborn need to make things.

  • Websites — custom HTML/CSS/JS, no templates, deployed and live
  • Photography — cars, events, street, nature, and everything else worth framing
  • Automotive Events — on the ground at Gumbal 3000 and HR Owen
  • Vibe Coding — building tools, apps, and experiments with Claude
  • Product Design — ideas that want to exist in the real world
